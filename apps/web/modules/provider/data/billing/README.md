# Provider billing and immutable documents

Financial commands use exact minor units, immutable source rows, an audit record and a durable Outbox event. A real receipt or payment file is reserved, uploaded privately and scanned by the trusted worker before money is recorded. Saved financial intents survive a browser refresh; only the original actor with a current billing role can resume or cancel an unfinished intent.

New statements and commission invoices require the shared approved issuer policy registry. Configuration and approval require two independent finance actors with MFA. Invoice numbers come from the same atomic issuer counter as Client invoices. Commission tax continues to use its own frozen Morocco tax-rule versions; the Client subscription policy tax rate does not replace it. No real identity, fiscal approval or production numbering configuration is seeded.

Financial sources wait in `EDITION_PENDING` until the emission consumer archives both FR and AR PDFs. Issuer, recipient, source hash, numbering policy and issue date are frozen. `ProviderBillingDocumentsReadyV1` then enters the notification queue. A queued email is distinct from a delivered email; verified active billing membership, preferences, consent and transport receipts still apply. An arbitrary accounting email grants no access.

`GET /api/provider/billing/{invoice|statement}/{uuid}?locale=fr|ar` first reads the source under the caller's RLS, obtains the frozen approved policy, and serves the original bytes after SHA-256 verification. Missing policy returns `503 BILLING_POLICY_REQUIRED`; no environment fallback invents an issuer. Older unapproved archives remain immutable evidence, and their source stays `LEGACY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` until an independent finance review can bind an applicable approved policy and valid historical number. Re-rendering never overwrites the older file.

Qualification migrations quarantine old evidence until a trusted scan matches its actual hash. Existing VERIFIED metadata is preserved but cannot enable new RFQ opportunities until CLEAN. Files without a governed reservation require controlled re-upload. Scan failures retain retry/dead-letter evidence. The production scanner, authorized finance settings and actual provider/transport accounts must be configured and exercised on staging; local tests do not claim that external acceptance.

Migration 253 intentionally does not manufacture identity approvals for legacy companies. A previous `VERIFIED` decision without a canonical identity anchor blocks new opportunities, even if an administrator later sets the organization to `ACTIVE`. Qualification displays that review requirement and links to document updates and authorized messaging. A Compliance administrator with MFA must inspect the current legal identity, ICE/IF/RC and the three current CLEAN reviewed documents, then submit the explicit version/hash-checked revalidation command. It records an immutable identity review. Existing missions and financial evidence remain readable; suspended or archived organizations are not automatically reactivated.
